About Me

Hi I’m Ad, a game programmer interested in gameplay, systems and the tools that help bring games to life.
Welcome to my portfolio where I showcase the projects I’ve worked on throughout my academic, independent & professional career. You’ll find a mix of game and tool projects. Some projects are finished while others are works in progress or explorations of an idea.
I graduated as a Game Programmer from Breda University of Applied Sciences studying Creative Media and Game Technologies, where I had the opportunity to work on a wide range of game development projects and collaborate with people from different disciplines. Through my own company Re:Shift Games, I’ve continued working on my own projects and exploring ideas through it.
Have a look around and see what I’ve been working on. And if you have any questions feel free to contact me.
